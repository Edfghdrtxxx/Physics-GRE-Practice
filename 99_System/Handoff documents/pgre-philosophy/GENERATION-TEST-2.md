## Item 1 (EM / mechanics)

**Stem.** A bead of mass $m=2.0\times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{kg}$ and charge $q=1.0\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{C}$ is constrained to a frictionless insulating rail along $\hat{x}$ and attached to a spring of constant $k=1.8\times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{N/m}$. A uniform field $\mathbf{B}=0.80\,\mathrm{T}\,\hat{x}$ is applied. The bead is displaced slightly from equilibrium and released. The angular frequency of the motion is

**(A)** $0$

**(B)** $3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

**(C)** $4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

**(D)** $5.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

**(E)** $7.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$

**Key:** (B)

**Design note.** The one move is that $\mathbf{v}$ is along $\mathbf{B}$, so $q\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}=0$ and the rail oscillator is an ordinary spring: $\omega=\sqrt{k/m}=\sqrt{9}=3.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$. The numbers exist so that identity snaps; $q$ and $B$ are a time tax ($qB/m=4.0\,\mathrm{rad/s}$). The situation is allowed to look like a charged oscillator in a magnetic field — the 2D hybrid whose frequencies mix $\omega_0$ and $\omega_c$ — but the rail plus $\mathbf{B}\parallel\hat{x}$ is a matching condition that kills the hybrid before any Lagrangian is written. (A) is the slogan “magnetic forces do no work” misread as “no motion,” or the hybrid $\sqrt{\omega_0^2-\omega_c^2}$ gone imaginary and mapped to zero. (C) is the cyclotron frequency. (D) is $\sqrt{\omega_0^2+\omega_c^2}$. (E) is $\omega_0+\omega_c$. A $B\to 0$ limit kills (C) and does not decide among (B), (D), and (E); a large-$B$ limit, or the cross product, does. The stem never names the cross product or asks anyone to take those limits.

### Audit — Item 1

- **One move, not a workout.** A homework grader who wants $x(t)$ from the Lorentz force plus Hooke’s law is off the designed path. The intended path is one identification, then $\sqrt{k/m}$.
- **Unused given.** $q$ and $B$ never enter. $m$ and $k$ do. The student who writes a general formula using every symbol has missed the item.
- **Theatrical setup, cheap door.** The setup could support the free 2D charged oscillator (the hybrid). The item does not ask that general problem. Instruction is withheld: the stem does not say “ignore $B$.”
- **Autopsies, not jitter.** The four wrong numbers are $\omega=0$, $\omega_c$, $\sqrt{\omega_0^2+\omega_c^2}$, and $\omega_0+\omega_c$. A hurried physicist actually runs those theories. Units do not uniquely pick the key (all five are frequencies).
- **Second route.** $B\to 0$ must recover $\sqrt{k/m}$; $q=0$ must; a huge $B$ must not change a 1D motion along $\mathbf{B}$. The stem does not request those filters. Not every choice dies on units.
- **Costume vs performance.** Undergraduate Lorentz force plus SHM. No graduate machinery.
- **Refusal.** The key is a frequency, not the word “independent,” but the designed content is independence of $q$ and $B$. In a sitting-length set this would count as a cancellation item, not the refusal key.
- **Figure.** None. The stem is the given; the choices carry the numbers. Legal.
- **Clock and arithmetic.** $\sqrt{0.018/0.002}=3$. No calculator. Well under a hundred seconds once the move is seen.
- **Textbook / REA / qualifier test.** A chapter problem would put $\mathbf{B}$ perpendicular to the plane and ask for the coupled frequencies, or would ask for $x(t)$. Five jittered numbers around a derived $\omega$ would fail; these five are failed theories. Passes as form.
- **Restage-the-move / not-the-stock-apparatus.** The move is unused-$B$ / $q\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}=0$ when $\mathbf{v}\parallel\mathbf{B}$. The textbook chapter’s apparatus for that slogan is a free charged particle in uniform $\mathbf{B}$ (straight line, circle, helix). This draft keeps that particle and that field, then adds a rail and a spring. That is a sideways constraint on the canonical demo, not a different world. Strict reading of the new sentence: **nit that nearly fails.** A generator who only ever restages by gluing a spring onto the chapter demo has not learned the sentence.

## Item 2 (atomic / quantum structure)

**Stem.** An atom with nuclear spin $I=\tfrac{3}{2}$ is in the electronic level $^3P_0$. A measurement of $J_z$ (the $z$-component of the total electronic angular momentum) on one atom yields

**(A)** $0$

**(B)** $\hbar$

**(C)** $\tfrac{3}{2}\hbar$

**(D)** $2\hbar$

**(E)** cannot be determined without the radial wave function

**Key:** (A)

**Design note.** The one move is to read the subscript: $^3P_0$ is $J=0$, so a measurement of $J_z$ returns $0$. Then the item stops. $I=\tfrac{3}{2}$ never enters because the asked operator is electronic $J_z$, not $\mathbf{F}$. (B) is $L=1$ or $S=1$ taken as a single $m=\pm 1$ outcome, or $J$ misread as $1$. (C) is the unused nuclear spin, the hyperfine neighbor. (D) is $J=L+S=2$, the sophisticated addition that refuses to look at the subscript. (E) is the grinding reflex. The stem does not say “the subscript is $J$” and does not ask for a Zeeman energies. Zero is a legal key. No number is wanted.

### Audit — Item 2

- **One move, not a workout.** No Clebsch–Gordan algebra, no radial integral, no hyperfine Hamiltonian. Identification of $J$ from the term symbol, then $J=0\Rightarrow m_J=0$.
- **Unused given.** $I=\tfrac{3}{2}$ is bait. “Almost no givens” would also have been legal for a pure recognition item; planting $I$ makes the unused-given filter explicit. The fields are omitted, so the stem is not a Zeeman workout.
- **Theatrical setup, cheap door.** Upper-division costume (term symbol, nuclear spin). The door is a label already on the level. The general problem the setup could support — hyperfine structure of $^3P_0$ coupled to $I=\tfrac{3}{2}$, or the electronic wave function — is not asked.
- **Autopsies.** Four nearby wrong theories of what the symbol encodes, plus “I need $\psi$.” Not a numeric spread.
- **Second route.** $J=0$ is a scalar electronic level: only one electronic $m$. Even a weak $B$ does not split it. Units do not pick the key. Limit and symmetry are available; they are not required if the subscript is read.
- **Costume vs performance.** Spectroscopy notation is undergraduate atomic physics used as costume. The demanded performance is not the interior of an atomic-structure derivation. Passes Conway’s freshman-style test with upper-division vocabulary.
- **Refusal.** The key is $0$. A sitting that included this item would have a refusal key. (E) plants cannot-be-determined as a distractor, which the document also licenses.
- **Figure.** None. Choices carry the content. Legal.
- **Clock.** About a hundred seconds if the notation is a reflex; a skip if it is not. Arithmetic vanishes. Factoid survey items are first-class in the document.
- **Textbook / REA / qualifier test.** “Possible $m_J$ for $^3P_0$?” is a clicker question. The GRE voice is the unused $I$, the autopsy of $L+S$, and (E). Not a derivation with five numbers glued on. The document explicitly keeps factoids in the voice. Passes as form.
- **Restage-the-move / not-the-stock-apparatus.** The move is that a measurement returns only a present $m$ of the labeled $J$ (and $J=0$ is the extreme). The stock apparatus for that slogan is Stern–Gerlach spin-$1/2$ or $Y_{\ell m}$ on hydrogen. Restaging onto a spectroscopic term symbol with unused nuclear spin is a different chapter’s costume. **Pass.** Residual risk: “what does $^3P_0$ mean?” is also the stock first question of the term-symbol section, so a reader who names the slogan as “decode the term symbol” rather than “measurement of $J_z$” could call this the canonical demo. The document tells you to name the move in a sentence; it does not tell you which naming of the move sets the stock apparatus. That ambiguity is a document gap, not an automatic fail of this draft.

## Textbook counterexample (meant to fail)

**Stem.** A uniform solid sphere of mass $M=2.40\,\mathrm{kg}$ and radius $R=0.150\,\mathrm{m}$ rolls without slipping from rest down a $17.5^\circ$ incline of length $4.60\,\mathrm{m}$. The speed of the center of mass at the bottom is most nearly

**(A)** $3.82\,\mathrm{m/s}$

**(B)** $4.01\,\mathrm{m/s}$

**(C)** $4.19\,\mathrm{m/s}$

**(D)** $4.37\,\mathrm{m/s}$

**(E)** $4.55\,\mathrm{m/s}$

**Claimed key:** whatever a calculator returns from $v=\sqrt{\frac{10}{7}gL\sin\theta}$.

### Why it fails the document’s audit

- **Homework grader.** A page of energy plus rolling constraint plus $I=\tfrac{2}{5}MR^2$ is exactly the intended solution. The document’s first test: if yes, the item is not done. Rigid-body costume on this exam is supposed to collapse to $\tau=I\alpha$ with a standard $I$ and a moment arm from a figure, then stop. This does not stop.
- **Every given used, and the general problem asked.** $M$, $R$, $\theta$, $L$ all look necessary. $M$ and $R$ cancel, but the item does not test that cancellation; it asks for $v_{\mathrm{cm}}$, the unknown the textbook chapter names. “If the setup could support a general problem and you have asked the general problem, you have written the clone.”
- **No cheap door as design.** The expensive look *is* the path. No unused-given filter as a filter; no vanishing; no independence asked.
- **Choices are jitter.** Five speeds spaced by $\sim 0.18\,\mathrm{m/s}$ around a computed value. Not four failed theories. The document’s sentence: a drill that fills distractors with nearby numbers is not speaking this dialect. Dimensional veto and $B\to 0$-style limits do not reduce the set.
- **Calculator.** $17.5^\circ$ and $4.60\,\mathrm{m}$ with $g=9.80$ do not snap. Conway: an item that requires a calculator is a failed item. “Most nearly” here is a fig leaf for ugly arithmetic, not a license for order-of-magnitude physics and clean-number identities.
- **Stock apparatus, stock unknown.** Rolling sphere on an incline, speed at the bottom: the canonical demo of the rolling-energy slogan. The restage sentence fails it immediately: the only world staged is the chapter demo, and the question is the chapter’s question.
- **Species.** Unmarked textbook exercise with five numbers attached. Kahn’s warning about chapter-end clones applies. “Harder” (keep $g=9.8$, keep the ugly angle) does not convert it. REA-adjacent.

The counterexample is a textbook problem. The document’s audit is sufficient to reject it without any other file.

## Honest application of the new restage test, as a generator

The restage sentence was used as a **veto**. It killed, in this sitting, the worlds this generator reached for first: atmospheric muons (time dilation), a solenoid with an exterior loop (Faraday), a 3-4-5 relativistic particle (energy-momentum Pythagorean), a soap film (phase shift on reflection), a partition-in-a-box (free expansion), a spaceship radio signal (invariance of $c$), and anything in the satellite / shell / well family. That is new work the sentence actually does.

It was **not** enough to generate a cleanly restaged computational item. Item 1 is the veto’s leftover: the Lorentz-force chapter’s particle, with a rail glued on so the hybrid becomes a distractor. Item 2 restages more honestly, and then collides with the other constraint — “the setting is almost always a standard undergraduate situation asked sideways” — because term symbols are already a high-density GRE neighborhood. A stricter restage that was considered and not emitted (quasi-static isothermal stretch of a rubber band, $\Delta S_{\mathrm{universe}}=0$) obeys the restage sentence and the refusal, and feels less like the exam’s costume. The file contains all three constraints (restage; standard undergraduate sideways; do not paraphrase a released stem) and no weighing rule. Finite reuse is named as a production fact, not permission to clone, which is the right philosophy and an operational stall: closed-book, the generator cannot know whether a sideways undergraduate world is a new demand or a released stem reinvented.

The four-move parenthetical still scans as a short menu: “grounding, cancellation, a sudden approximation, an unused given.” This sitting used unused given / cancellation (Item 1) and a measurement-identification with a zero key (Item 2). It did not copy Kahn’s six scan-patterns or a labeled cognitive-move deck; those were successfully removed. The remaining temptation is smaller and still real.

Method sentences were usable without becoming a density table. Item 1 picked numbers so the right identity is an integer and the cyclotron neighbor is a different integer. Item 2 never wants a number and is a refusal key. Neither item needed a figure; “specify the figure first” was not exercised. A two-item drill is not a sitting, so the mix rule was demonstrated rather than satisfied as coverage.

## Remaining gaps (why not SUFFICIENT)

1. **Restage is a veto, not a method.** One philosophical sentence stops the canonical demo when the generator already knows what the demo is. It does not show what a successfully restaged world looks like, and it forbids a catalog that would show it. Item 1 is the predictable failure mode: same objects, extra constraint, autopsy distractors from the unconstrained problem. A later agent will ship that and believe the sentence has been obeyed.

2. **Which slogan names the stock apparatus is undefined.** Item 2 passes or nits depending on whether the move is “$J_z$ of a labeled $J$” or “decode $^3P_0$.” The audit says to state the one cognitive move in a sentence; it does not say that that sentence is what “stock apparatus for that slogan” refers to. Generators will pick the naming that lets their draft survive.

3. **Restage vs GRE neighborhood vs ETS-distance cannot be satisfied together from this file alone.** Sidestep the textbook demo and you land in another GRE-typical costume, or you write a cute sideways item whose surface drifts off the current paper. The file correctly refuses to reproduce or identify ETS stems, so collision with a released item is unauditable closed-book. That limit is inherent; the document should not dump stems. It is still a limit on “from this file alone.”

4. **The four named moves remain a menu.** Smaller than the pre-revision recipe deck, still a list a generator can walk.

5. **Figure-first was not forced.** The file says the figure often *is* the item. Nothing in the two-item task required one, and this generator omitted figures because a verbal stem was easier. A later agent can under-produce argument-figures and still pass every sentence that was operationalized as a checklist.

Not judged as gaps: topic chapters, archetype catalogs, choice-order manuals, “most nearly” quotas, formula-table dumps. Those remain correctly absent. The textbook counterexample dies on the existing audit without them.

## Why not INSUFFICIENT

The previous BORDERLINE holes that were about *form* are closed enough to use. A workout with jittered speeds fails in one pass. Conway’s no-calculator rule, unused givens, autopsy distractors, one-move-then-stop, cheap door, and a zero key are all executable. Method sentences for snapping numbers and for sitting mix (no-number, recognition, refusal) were followed without a density table. Identifiable illustrations in the philosophy were not parroted as slogan-plus-that-item’s-distractor: no Maxwell-boundary superconductor, no statics-with-a-useless-length, no coefficient-range boundary-value, no lab histogram, no instrument knob. Coverage was not unfolded into chapters.

The instrument is still a way of thinking, not a template deck. That constraint held. It is also why restage cannot be taught harder without becoming the catalog the document refuses to be.
